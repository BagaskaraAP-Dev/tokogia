# Project Guidelines · Gia's Patisserie

## Anti-Slop Guidelines (Active)
This project uses the **antislop** suite to ensure clean, human-crafted design, authentic copywriting, and code hygiene:
- **Core Filter:** `antislop` (Always active to prevent generic AI patterns)
- **UI & Visual:** `antislop-ui` (Crafted typography, intentional palettes, authentic layout, restrained motion)
- **Mobile & Responsive:** `antislop-layoutmobile` (Mobile-first, iOS Safari safe-areas, proper tap targets)
- **Accessibility & Human:** `antislop-human` (High contrast, focus rings, keyboard accessibility)
- **Code Hygiene:** `antislop-code` (Meaningful comments only, no generic AI-slop comments)
- **Copywriting:** `antislop-copywriting` (Warm, authentic pastry shop voice)

## Brand & Design Identity
- **Brand:** Gia's Patissèrie (Homemade with Love · Freshly Baked Cookies)
- **Colors:** Soft bakery pink, deep berry/burgundy accents (`#d0346d`, `#6d0f2a`), crisp white cards (`#ffffff`), and warm pastel background (`#fff5f8`).
- **Typography:** Fraunces (serif display for headings) + Plus Jakarta Sans (clean sans-serif for UI & numbers).
- **Target Platform:** Mobile-first PWA optimized for iPhone Safari Home Screen standalone use.
